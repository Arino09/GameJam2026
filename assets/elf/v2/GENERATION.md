# 精灵 v2

使用内置 imagegen 生成新素材；用 sprite-pipeline 做统一缩放、脚底和头部对齐、统一 48 色调色板及逐帧预览。每个方向的 8 帧共用缩放系数，不单独拉伸各帧。右向使用左向镜像。

运行资源：`elf_atlas.png`（1152×512）、`elf_frames.tres`。每帧 128×128，可见高度约 120 像素；在世界中高约 60 单位，保持 32×32 地图尺度。1280×800 渲染视口、2 倍镜头与角色 0.5 倍世界缩放让默认窗口中的角色素材像素与屏幕像素一一对应。

行走为 8 帧、正常速度等效 14 FPS，奔跑约 17.6 FPS。动画按实际位移推进，转向保持步伐相位，停下后切换待机。

重建：`python tools/build_elf_v2.py --pipeline <sprite-pipeline 所在插件的 scripts 目录>`，需要 Pillow。检查产物在 `build/qa/elf_v2/`。

## 基础造型提示词

Use case: style-transfer. Image 1 is only a character identity/costume reference. Create ONE production PIXEL ART GAME SPRITE of this blonde adult elf adventurer facing DOWN toward the viewer, in a calm neutral standing pose with both feet planted. True top-down RPG 3/4 overhead view showing top of head, not isometric. MUST be a deliberately low-resolution 64x64 sprite shown at exactly 16x nearest-neighbor enlargement on a 1024x1024 canvas. Each native pixel is a solid 16x16 block! Character about 34 native pixels wide and 58 native pixels tall, feet at native y60, head top y3. Readable 3.5-head stylized RPG proportions, larger clear face, adult adventurer. Simplify the reference into LARGE CLEAN COLOR CLUSTERS, not fine illustration: golden blonde hair with two clearly separated thick braids, long elf ears, one violet hair flower, ivory tunic dress with simple asymmetrical skirt, lavender short cape and sleeves, dark plum belt and boots. Face readable at actual 64 pixels: two distinct violet eyes, uninterrupted light skin plane, clear golden bangs, no strands across face. Restricted 24-color palette, 3 shades per material. Solid interior colors, dark purple outer contour, no dithering, no fabric texture, no noise, no intricate lace, no tiny scattered highlights, no gradients, no antialiasing. Charming polished 16-bit RPG sprite craft. Centered character alone, complete head-to-boots, transparent PNG alpha background; no floor, no shadow, no halo, no text, no frame. Make it look intentionally authored at native game resolution, never a detailed portrait reduced to pixels.

Create ONE COMPLETE 8-frame WALK CYCLE of this exact same adult elf for an overhead 2D RPG. Every cell must contain exactly one COMPLETE head-to-boots sprite at same scale. Render the elf about 180px wide and 440px tall, center x192 and soles y480 in each cell, head around y40. Character will be normalized to a 128x128 game frame with visible height120px. Keep the approved seed's identity: golden thick twin braids, long pointed ears, large clear violet eyes, one violet flower, simple ivory asymmetrical dress panels, short lavender cape/sleeves, dark plum boots. Same head size and torso proportions in all eight frames. Deliberately clean large pixel clusters, restrained 24-32-color palette, clear dark plum silhouette outline, NO texture noise, NO tiny speckles, no antialiasing or blur, no gradient, no floor shadow. Transparent alpha across all empty pixels including limb gaps, not black backdrop. No text, labels, cell borders or background. WALK TIMING: 1 left foot forward contact and right arm forward; 2 weight down, left foot supports; 3 right leg passing with right knee bent and left arm moving forward; 4 high position right foot swinging forward; 5 right foot forward contact with left arm forward; 6 weight down right foot supports; 7 left leg passing with left knee bent; 8 high position left foot swinging forward, smoothly returning to frame1. Opposite arm swings, modest 1-2 native pixel body bob, braids/cape trailing with slight delayed sway. All EIGHT poses must differ with clearly alternating leading legs; don't merely repeat two poses. Keep head orientation locked throughout, no spinning. Do not copy the standing pose eight times.
Facing direction: every frame faces DOWN / towards viewer, front-facing with the same visible face as approved seed. This is walking toward the camera, with fore-shortened opposing legs.
